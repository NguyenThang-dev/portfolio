## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-clarity, forward-looking aesthetic tailored for an artificial intelligence researcher and student portfolio. It bridges academic rigor with high-craft digital product design, projecting technical authority, intellectual curiosity, and approachable elegance.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Expression
The visual language merges **Modern Tech Minimalism** with refined **Luminous Glassmorphism**. The interface relies on ultra-clean spatial distribution, crisp typography, and disciplined layouts, enriched by soft ambient chromatic gradients that bleed seamlessly into translucent surfaces.

- **Atmosphere:** Clean, open, crystalline, and illuminated.
- **Surface Texture:** Frosted glass panes, semi-translucent backdrops, and delicate high-key inner rim lighting (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`).
- **Tone:** Academic excellence, technical precision, balanced optimism, and polished poise.
- **Linguistic Inclusivity:** Optimized explicitly for bilingual presentation (English and Vietnamese), prioritizing typographic balance, stable vertical metric headroom for tone marks, and clean glyph cadence across mixed-language abstracts and technical documentation.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an energetic, high-trust cobalt foundation, complemented by atmospheric secondary and accent hues that form the core chromatic glow.

### Color Roles & Ratios
- **Primary (`#3B82F6` - Cobalt Blue):** Anchors brand recognition, critical CTAs, active pagination steps, and primary focus rings.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Electric Cyan):** Denotes secondary highlights, metrics visualizations, progress indicators, and high-energy focal nodes.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` - Electric Violet / Soft Lilac):** Signals academic classifications, generative AI tag categories, paper citations, and delicate gradient endpoints.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate):** Drives neutral step scales, technical copy, captions, structural outlines, and structural layout scaffolding.

### Ambient Radial Gradients
The canvas employs fixed ambient light sources positioned strategically behind glass cards to create subtle dimensional luminescence:
- **Gradient Aura 1:** Soft radial blend of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)` (Cyan) fading to zero at 600px radius.
- **Gradient Aura 2:** Subtle accent blend of `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.10)` (Electric Violet) blending across card clusters.
- **Surface Translucency:** Card fill relies on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` over canvas base `#F8FAFC`.

## Typography

The typography pairs geometric, forward-leaning headings (**Plus Jakarta Sans**) with systematic, neutral analytical body copy (**Inter**).

### Dual Script & Vietnamese Typesetting Rules
- **Diacritical Safe Zones:** Vietnamese diacritics (dấu sắc, huyền, hỏi, ngã, nặng, and combined circumflex/horn marks) require sufficient vertical clearance. Heading line heights must never fall below `1.25x` font size, and body line heights must maintain at least `1.5x` font size to avoid visual clipping of stacked diacritics.
- **Kerning & Tracking:** Heading tracking is restricted between `-0.01em` and `-0.03em`. Excessively tight tracking causes collisions between tone marks and adjacent uppercase ascenders.
- **Monospace Integration:** Inline code snippets, model hyperparameters, benchmark scores, and arXiv citations deploy `JetBrains Mono` with identical baseline alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural framework is built on an adaptive **12-column fluid grid** centered within a maximum container width of `1280px`.

### Responsive Grid Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `margin-lg` (4rem / 64px), `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). Allows structured multi-column project cards, thesis sidebars, and analytical metric grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Reflows dual-pane case studies into vertical stacks while preserving multi-item chip groupings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Cards collapse to 100% container width with horizontal scroll carousels for chip tags and auxiliary media.

### Spatial Rhythms
Element gaps adhere strictly to the 4px/8px micro-grid. Generous section spacing (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` for major divider sections) maintains the open, contemplative whitespace typical of elevated scientific publications.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphic surfaces coupled with diffuse ambient drop shadows rather than heavy structural borders.

### Surface System
1. **Canvas Level:** Foundation tint `#F8FAFC` overlaid with wide-diffusion, static mesh gradients (cyan, cobalt, and lilac at 8-12% opacity).
2. **Surface Low (Glass Substrates):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`, backdrop-filter `blur(12px) saturate(180%)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`. Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Surface Raised (Interactive Cards & Modals):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, backdrop-filter `blur(20px) saturate(190%)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)`. Subtle top-edge inner highlight: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
4. **Surface Floating (Tooltips, Floating Nav, Popovers):** Solid/semi-solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, backdrop-filter `blur(24px)`. Shadow: `0 20px 40px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

### Inner Rim Lighting Principle
All elevated components apply a refined top/left border gradient simulating directional light passing through bevelled crystal glass, transitioning from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` at top-left to `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.40)` at bottom-right.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape identity. This balance avoids both sterile hyper-rectangles and overly casual bubbled radiuses, reflecting mathematical clarity and organic approachability.

### Radius Scale Implementation
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- **Cards & Research Panels:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- **Hero Containers, Media Viewports & Modals:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- **Badges, Status Tags, Pill Navigation & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid cobalt `#3B82F6` text white, `rounded-md` (0.5rem), subtle top inner highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`. Hover: `#2563EB` with shadow `0 6px 20px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary (Glass):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Zero fill, text `#475569`. Hover: background `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.06)`, text `#3B82F6`.
- **Micro-interaction:** Standard duration `150ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, scale down to `0.98` on click.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Status Badges:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, text `label-sm`.
- **Topic Taxonomy:** AI model tags (e.g., "Transformer", "Vision-Language", "PyTorch") use soft lilac/cyan tinted surfaces: background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.20)`, text `#6D28D9`.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Toggleable glass pills that switch to solid primary `#3B82F6` with white typography when active.

### Cards (Portfolio & Research)
- **Structure:** Encased in translucent white `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`. 
- **Hover State:** Translate-Y `-3px`, ambient shadow expands `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.12)`, border highlight brightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`.
- **Anatomy:** Header contains date, publication venue badge, and language toggle; body contains research abstract with strict typographic rhythm; footer includes arXiv link, code repository button, and collaborator avatars.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Base Style:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `0.625rem 1rem`, `rounded-md`.
- **Focus State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `#3B82F6`, ring `3px solid rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`. Transition `200ms ease`.
- **Bilingual Placeholders:** Support clean typography for mixed ASCII and accented UTF-8 placeholder strings without vertical layout jitter.

### Lists & Research Indices
- **Divider:** Subtle gradient lines fading from `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0)` through `rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.8)` to `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0)`.
- **Publication Row:** Hovering highlights the row with `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.5)` background and reveals action buttons (PDF, BibTeX, Demo).

### Language Switcher (EN / VI)
- Glass segment pill control displaying active item on a raised white rounded thumb (`rounded-full`) with delicate shadow `0 2px 6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Smooth horizontal sliding indicator transitions with spring dynamics (`damping: 24`, `stiffness: 300`).
